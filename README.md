# Hi, I'm Jaemin Jeon 👋

I am an undergraduate student in **Electrical Engineering at Korea University**.

My research interests center on **reliable information recovery from noisy physical systems**, spanning surface-code quantum error correction, machine-learning-assisted decoding, and high-speed wireline communications.

As an Undergraduate Researcher in the **Communications & Information Systems Lab**, I developed a transition-aware neural pre-decoder for surface-code quantum error correction. The model selects local detector-event edits before PyMatching while retaining PyMatching as the final logical-frame decoder.

- **Neural Equalization and Sequence Detection** for High-Speed Wireline Links

### 🧪 Featured Research

#### Transition-Aware Neural Pre-Decoder for Surface-Code QEC

- Designed a neural pre-decoder using a **3D residual network**, local detector-edit candidates, benefit/harm ranking, and safety-gated adoption
- Generated rotated surface-code datasets with **Stim** for code distances \(d = 3, 5, 7\) under heterogeneous and correlated noise models
- Improved mean held-out logical-frame accuracy over raw **PyMatching** by **+0.66 pp at \(d = 3\)** and **+0.56 pp at \(d = 5\)**
- Identified limited selector generalization under noise-family shift as the primary bottleneck at \(d = 7\)
