# Hi, I'm Jaemin Jeon 👋

I am an undergraduate student in **Electrical Engineering at Korea University** and an **incoming M.S. student at KAIST**, starting in **Spring 2027**. I will join the **CoCoA (Coding and Communications Lab)**, led by **Prof. Jeongseok Ha**.

My research interests center on **reliable information recovery from noisy physical systems**, with a focus on quantum and classical error correction, machine-learning-assisted decoding, and efficient decoder implementation.

As an undergraduate researcher in the **Communications & Information Systems Lab**, I developed a transition-aware neural pre-decoder for surface-code quantum error correction. The model selects local detector-event edits before PyMatching while retaining PyMatching as the final logical-frame decoder.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Quantum Error Correction:** Surface-code decoding and fault-tolerant quantum computing
- **Classical Error-Correcting Codes:** Coding and decoding for communication and storage systems
- **Machine-Learning-Assisted Decoding:** Robustness to correlated noise and distribution shift
- **Efficient Decoder Implementation:** Low-latency, low-complexity, and hardware-aware design
- **High-Speed Wireline Communications:** Neural equalization and sequence detection

### 🧪 Featured Research

#### Transition-Aware Neural Pre-Decoder for Surface-Code QEC

- Designed a neural pre-decoder using a **3D residual network**, local detector-edit candidates, benefit/harm ranking, and safety-gated adoption
- Generated rotated surface-code datasets with **Stim** for code distances **3, 5, and 7** under heterogeneous and correlated noise models
- Improved mean held-out logical-frame accuracy over raw **PyMatching** by **0.66 percentage points at distance 3** and **0.56 percentage points at distance 5**
- Identified limited selector generalization under noise-family shift as the primary bottleneck at **distance 7**
